# EMS Patient Registration – Interactive Prototype

Open `index.html` in a modern browser on a phone or computer. Select Paramedic to add a **fictional** case, Reception to change registration or patient status, or Admin to see statistics, search and export a demo CSV.

## IMPORTANT — NOT FOR REAL PATIENT DATA
This is a **front-end-only prototype**. Role selection is not authentication; localStorage is not a secure medical database; there is no server, encryption at rest, real authorization, audit integrity, HIS integration, or approved document upload. Do not enter real patient information, IDs, visas, photos or medical records.

## Production work needed
Hospital IT/security and privacy review; approved hosting and database; server-side role authorization; employee identity/MFA; secure direct camera upload without local photo persistence; encrypted object storage; scoped case access; immutable audit logs; retention/deletion policies; notifications without PHI; HIS integration; operational monitoring; backups; penetration testing and deployment.
